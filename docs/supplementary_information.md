## Supplementary Figure Legends

### Supplementary Figure S1. Immature-versus-mature granule-cell stage split.

**a,** Stage-split module scores for dentate and cerebellar granule candidates. Values are median rank-like module scores across immature and mature/maturing bins, with rows showing progenitor, neuronal maturation, neurite/morphology, synaptic/excitability, neurogenic niche, TGF-beta/SMAD, and BDNF/ERK modules. **b,** Cross-branch similarity by stage, computed as 1 - |cerebellum - dentate| for each module. Higher values indicate more similar dentate and cerebellar module scores within the sampled stage bin. **c,** Mature-minus-immature similarity differences. Positive bars indicate modules whose dentate/cerebellar similarity is stronger in the mature or maturing bin, whereas negative bars indicate stronger similarity in the immature bin. The analysis is stage-aware but asymmetric because cerebellar explicit staging is primarily from GSE122357, while dentate staging is supported by multiple datasets.

### Supplementary Figure S2. Granule-enriched genes relative to named pyramidal and Purkinje comparators.

**a,** Granule-lineage screen. Bars show log1p expression deltas for genes that are higher in dentate granule-lineage groups than named pyramidal comparators in GSE104323 and higher in cerebellar granule-lineage groups than Purkinje cells in GSE122357. Blue bars show dentate granule > pyramidal delta; orange bars show cerebellar granule > Purkinje delta. **b,** Postmitotic/granule-cell state screen using a more restricted granule-cell-state contrast. NFIA, RBFOX3, and HMGN2 remain the most interpretable shared candidates, with NFIA providing the clearest regulatory candidate and RBFOX3 marking postmitotic neuronal maturation.

### Supplementary Figure S3. Regional-origin and shared-toolkit timing analysis.

**a-d,** Ordered dentate states from GSE104323, spanning radial glia-like/progenitor groups through neuroblast, immature granule, juvenile granule, and adult granule states. Panels show regional fate polarity, NEUROD1/RBFOX3 postmitotic-special rank, construction rank, and the combined configuration timing score. **e-h,** Ordered cerebellar states from GSE122357, spanning P0, P8a, and P8b granule precursor and granule-cell labels for the same four metrics. The analysis supports branch-specific regional fate timing with later or persistent maturation/construction components, consistent with distinct regional origins followed by partial reuse of a shared granule-cell maturation and construction toolkit.

### Supplementary Figure S4. Developmental-origin control for deep origin, regional divergence, and later toolkit reuse.

**a,** Conservative route model. Dentate granule cells are framed as arising through anterior neural tube/telencephalic and medial pallium/dentate-stream routes, whereas cerebellar granule cells are framed as arising through posterior neural tube/hindbrain and rhombic-lip/external-granule-layer routes. Both lineages can later reuse maturation and construction machinery. **b,c,** Marker-module audit in ordered dentate states from GSE104323 and cerebellar granule precursor/granule states from GSE122357. Values are within-sample module ranks, so high values indicate relative enrichment of that module within the sampled cell-state context. Dentate states lose deep neural-progenitor rank with maturation and show stronger medial pallium/dentate and construction ranks in postmitotic/immature windows. Cerebellar precursor states retain strong hindbrain/rhombic-lip ranks, while cerebellar granule states retain high shared postmitotic/construction ranks. **d,** Summary indices show branch-origin polarity and shared-convergence rank across ordered states. The panel supports a conservative model in which the common origin is deep neural ectoderm/neuroepithelium before regional patterning, followed by distinct regional trajectories and later toolkit reuse.

### Supplementary Figure S5. Hierarchical integrative model component-score summary.

Bars show term-balanced evidence scores for the five components of the hierarchical integrative model: direct transcriptomic configuration, stage/niche context, provisional regulatory-compatibility extension, morphology/activity calibration, and resource-constraint rank agreement. Scores range from -1 to 1, with positive values supporting the predicted contribution of each component to granule-like configuration or calibration.

### Supplementary Figure S6. Public perturbation module-shift signatures.

Heatmap of signed module-shift scores from public perturbation resources processed after perturbation-resource triage. Columns are perturbation contrasts from GSE242199 NTRK2 knockout/BDNF-response human neural progenitors, GSE71916 Rbfox1/3 knockdown hippocampal neurons, GSE81962 P6 cerebellar GNP Ptch/Ndp arrays, and GSE84786 Rbfox3 knockout hippocampus. Rows are curated granule convergence modules. Red values indicate positive module shifts in the perturbation or treatment condition relative to control; blue values indicate negative shifts. Scores combine median module-gene effect size with module-gene coverage.
